Summary# Growth Marketing Specialist

Remote | Full-Time**

HealthVue works with medical practices across Australia to improve performance, profitability, operations and growth.

We are looking for a **Growth Marketing Specialist** who can take an offer and turn it into a complete lead generation campaign.

This is not a social media posting role.

We are looking for someone who understands **campaigns, funnels, paid advertising, email marketing, automation and conversion**, and who is comfortable being measured on leads, appointments and revenue, not likes and followers.

## What you'll be responsible for

You will help build and manage HealthVue's marketing and lead generation engine.

You will be responsible for:

* Planning and launching marketing campaigns
* Building lead generation funnels
* Creating landing pages and forms
* Managing campaigns inside GoHighLevel
* Building automated email and SMS nurture sequences
* Managing and segmenting our CRM database
* Creating database reactivation campaigns
* Setting up lead follow-up workflows
* Managing Meta advertising campaigns
* Supporting Google Ads campaigns where required
* Building retargeting campaigns
* Creating and testing campaign offers, hooks and calls to action
* Working with our content team to turn content into campaigns
* Developing lead magnets, assessments, calculators, webinars and other acquisition strategies
* Tracking leads from initial enquiry through to booked appointments
* Monitoring campaign performance and identifying opportunities to improve conversion
* Reporting weekly on leads, cost per lead, bookings, conversion and pipeline generated

## How our sales process works

You will not be responsible for closing sales.

Your job is to **generate demand and create qualified opportunities**.

Our marketing campaigns generate and nurture leads.

Our Appointment Setter then follows up engaged prospects, qualifies them and books appointments.

Our CEO handles the sales conversation.

Your responsibility is making sure that pipeline is consistently being fed.

## What success looks like

We care about commercial outcomes.

Your performance will ultimately be measured through:

**Campaigns → Leads → Qualified Leads → Booked Appointments → Attended Appointments → Opportunities → Clients → Revenue**

We want someone who looks at a campaign and asks:

**"How many qualified opportunities did this generate?"**

Not:

**"How many likes did this post get?"**
